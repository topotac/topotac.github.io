Understanding the pressure lines

Static (S): Estimated hose pressure when no water is flowing; accounts for elevation changes.

Flowing (F): Estimated hose pressure while flowing the calculated GPM at a pump discharge pressure of 150 PSI. Accounts for elevation, hose friction, and modeled wye losses.

Planning estimate only. MARK-3 reference flow; 1½-inch hose and a gated wye per 100 ft. Elevation comes from USGS 3DEP raster; elevations are sampled every 50 ft, but smaller terrain low spots can still be missed. Actual flow, hose friction, nozzle demand, appliance losses and pump shutoff pressure may differ. Flows below 100 or above 250 PSI are extrapolated beyond MARK-3 reference points. 300 PSI is a planning alert, not a hose rating. Closed valves and water hammer can produce higher pressures.
